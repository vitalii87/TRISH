# TRISH

**Trans-Reality Information Substrate Hypothesis**  
By **Vitalii Zhyliaiev** · Version **0.1** · October 28, 2026

> **Genre and status:** A speculative philosophical essay with science-fiction elements. An exploratory work in progress, not an established physical theory or a claim of scientific discovery.

**[Read the full essay →](TRISH.md)**

## About

TRISH explores a thought experiment: could an intelligence within a constructed world understand the foundations supporting both its world and its creators' world better than its creators do?

Its central proposition is:

> The hierarchy of creation is not necessarily the hierarchy of knowledge.

The essay distinguishes the origin of a reality, the capabilities of its inhabitants, and their possible access to underlying mechanisms. It considers a hypothetical shared informational substrate and the additional assumptions that interaction between realities would require.

The existence of such a substrate or interaction mechanism is not established. The essay currently offers no distinctive empirical predictions, and its scientific originality has not been established through a comprehensive literature review.
